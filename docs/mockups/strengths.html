Why this app

Most genealogy tools store conclusions and quietly overwrite them. This one stores the evidence and the reasoning as an append-only event log, then derives the family tree from it. The result: you can always see who claimed what, on what basis, and undo any of it. Below, each differentiator is shown with the actual component you’ll meet in the app.
