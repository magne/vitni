Same person as the 1865 census entry?

The 1865 census entry for Nordland and the parish register both give a birth year of 1850 for a John Smith, carpenter. The question is whether they are one person.

Points in favour:

  • Both name the same occupation and the same farm.
  • The register's baptism sponsor appears as a neighbour in the census household.

Against: the census spells the surname Smyth, and no probate links the two.

Conclusion: same person, Normal confidence. Cited from the parish register; a probate record would raise it.
